# 2.0.1 — Silent emotes and Astro Jack materials

**Recorded local delivery · 2026-09-16**

## Changes

- Removed emote audio extraction, exported audio files and Blender sound strips from new exports.
- Adjusted Business Hips cloth handling: separated coats and bodies, excluded residual coat weights from body simulation, disabled overlapping self-collision and added an inferred rest-shape tether.
- Added Astro Jack galaxy visor, face-expression and animated coat reconstruction with the source colour-curve atlas; SICKO MODE added the rotating planet texture.
- Based animated surface motion on elapsed seconds so changing FPS preserves effect speed.
- Excluded hidden vertex-crunch placeholder surfaces from rendering and missing-base-texture warnings.

## Validation and limitations

The archived notes explicitly retain Marge's raised-knee dress clipping. Material effects and physics are Blender reconstructions; arbitrary Niagara and gameplay reactions remain incomplete. Updating does not remove audio from older Blender files—re-export them.
